# DOC-GFDRR-003: Bangladesh Coastal Polder Embankment & Multi-Purpose Cyclone Shelter M&E Review

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-BD-2026-002`
- **Country / Region:** Bangladesh (`SAR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Resilient Infrastructure
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.60M / $520.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 8,200,000

## Executive Summary

M&E evaluation of storm-surge polder crest upgrades, tidal drainage sluices, and universal-access cyclone shelters in Khulna and Barishal divisions informing $520M in World Bank coastal infrastructure.

## S1.0 Cyclonic Surge & Saline Intrusion Risk Baseline (p.1-2)

Compound sea-level rise and Category-4 Bay of Bengal cyclone storm surges subject 19 coastal polders in Khulna and Barishal to overtopping probabilities above 8% per year, degrading rice cropping intensity through prolonged root-zone salinity.

## S2.0 Engineering Standards & Financing Informed (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-BD-2026-002 ($1.60M) updated dynamic wave-runup design standards and drainage gate automation specs, directly informing $520.0M under the Coastal Embankment Improvement Project Phase II across 610 km of embankment armouring and 95 dual-use school-shelters.

## S3.0 Results Framework Outcome Verification (p.6-8)

Contribution to GFDRR's 100M 2030 target is assessed at 8,200,000 direct coastal residents (RF-2030-IND-01). Evacuation travel time to an all-weather shelter fell from 54 minutes to 21 minutes median across surveyed unions.

## S4.0 Disability & Gender-Responsive Shelter Audits (p.9-10)

100% of newly financed shelters incorporate 1:12 wheelchair ramps, solar-lit gender-segregated WASH blocks, lactation rooms, and dedicated livestock plinths—lifting women's pre-landfall evacuation compliance in drill surveys from 58% to 86%.
