- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 1,400,000

## Executive Summary

Multi-hazard flood and extreme-heat vulnerability screening of primary health centers and maternal clinics across Hodh Ech Chargui and Nouakchott in Mauritania.

## S1.0 Flash Flood & Thermal Stress on Health Infrastructure (p.1-2)

Seasonal wadi flash floods and indoor ward temperatures exceeding 41°C disrupted cold-chain vaccine storage and obstetric care in 38% of surveyed clinics across Hodh Ech Chargui.

## S2.0 Health Facility Prioritization & Retrofitting Package (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-MR-2026-009 ($1.15M) screened 310 health posts and informed $120.0M in World Bank Health System Support financing for cool-roof insulation, solar battery cold-chains, and elevated perimeter flood drainage.

## S3.0 M&E Human Development Resilience Metrics (p.6-7)

Retrofitted maternal and primary clinics maintain 99.1% cold-chain uptime during grid outages and serve 1,400,000 catchment residents including Malian refugee host communities.
