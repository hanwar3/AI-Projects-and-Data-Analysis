# DOC-GFDRR-019: Pakistan Karachi & Jacobabad Extreme Urban Heat & Cooling Shelter Evaluation

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-PK-2027-019`
- **Country / Region:** Pakistan (`SAR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** City Resilience - Urban Heat Resilience
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.45M / $210.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 5,100,000

## Executive Summary

Urban heat stress micro-climate mapping, reflective lime-wash cool roof trials, and women-accessible municipal cooling hubs in Karachi and Jacobabad, Sindh.

## S1.0 Humid Heat Thresholds Approaching Physiological Limits (p.1-2)

Jacobabad and dense katchi abadi settlements in Karachi record 52+ annual days above 35°C dry-bulb and wet-bulb spikes exceeding 31°C, driving acute heatstroke surges during load-shedding blackouts.

## S2.0 Reflective Cool Roofs & Solar Cooling Hubs (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-PK-2027-019 ($1.45M) evaluated low-cost elastomeric and lime-pozzolana cool coatings alongside 65 solar-backed community cooling centers, informing $210.0M in Sindh Liveable Cities World Bank financing.

## S3.0 M&E Thermal Comfort & Labour Productivity Outcomes (p.6-7)

Indoor ceiling temperatures dropped by 3.4°C median in home-based garment workshops, protecting 5,100,000 urban residents under RF-2030-IND-02.

## S4.0 Household Sensor Survey & Enumerator Log (p.8-9)
