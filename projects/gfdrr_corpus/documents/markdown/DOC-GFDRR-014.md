## Executive Summary

Strictly Confidential FCV monitoring note on Shabelle-Juba flash-flood early warnings and protection safeguards for internally displaced person (IDP) settlements in Somalia.

## S1.0 Alternating Multi-Season Drought & Riverine Breakouts (p.1-2)

Informal IDP settlements sited on low-lying river terraces in Beletweyne, Baidoa, and peri-urban Mogadishu face sudden Shabelle embankment breaches following multi-year drought.

## S2.0 Telecom Warning Broadcasts & IDA Financing Informed (p.3-4)

Grant GFDRR-SO-2027-014 ($1.95M) established toll-free Somali dialect USSD/audio alerts and Shabelle telemetric radar gauges, informing $210.0M in IDA Water & Crisis Resilience operations.

## S3.0 M&E Reach & Protection Outcomes (p.5-6)

Verified alert reach covers 3,600,000 vulnerable and displaced persons (RF-2030-IND-06), with 81% of surveyed women camp leaders confirming receipt >= 48 hours prior to peak stage.
