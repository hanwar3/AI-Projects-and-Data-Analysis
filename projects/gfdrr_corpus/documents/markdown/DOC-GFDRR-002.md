- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 3,900,000

## Executive Summary

Confidential M&E third-party verification of plinth-elevated, lime-stabilized resilient housing reconstruction tranches in flood-impacted districts of Sindh and eastern Balochistan.

## S1.0 Structural Vulnerability & Housing Typology Audit (p.1-3)

Unreinforced mud-brick (katcha) dwellings suffered an 84% collapse rate where standing floodwaters exceeded 0.9 meters for more than 72 hours. Grant GFDRR-PK-2026-004 financed shake-table and soak-pit durability tests for burnt-brick and stabilized compressed earth block units with 0.75-meter raised masonry plinths and ring-beam ties.

## S2.0 Tranche Inspection & Financing Leverage (p.4-6)

Geotagged mobile inspection protocols informed tranche disbursement rules across $500.0M of IDA reconstruction co-financing. Plinth-level pass rates rose from 62.4% in Q1 FY2026 to 91.8% in Q3 FY2026 following deployment of vernacular pictorial mason guides.

## S3.0 M&E Results & Joint Land-Title Indicators (p.7-8)

Tracked under RF-2030-IND-03 (resilient housing units meeting multi-hazard code), 560,000 core units are verified on schedule, protecting 3,900,000 household members. Crucially, 71.2% of newly issued occupancy and reconstruction bank accounts were registered in women's names or joint spousal title.
