# DOC-GFDRR-020: Eastern Caribbean Crisis Preparedness Toolkit (CPRT) & Inclusive Shelter Audit

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-LAC-2027-020`
- **Country / Region:** Eastern Caribbean (`LAC`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 4: Improving Disaster Preparedness and Resilient Recovery
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Emergency Preparedness and Response
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.10M / $145.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 1,100,000

## Executive Summary

Application of the World Bank Crisis Preparedness and Response Toolkit (CPRT) across OECS small island states to close emergency logistics, telecom, and disability-shelter gaps.

## S1.0 Multi-Island Hurricane Logistics Bottlenecks (p.1-2)

Category-5 rapid-intensification hurricanes in the Eastern Caribbean can disable single deep-water ports and inter-island airfields simultaneously.

## S2.0 CPRT Readiness Diagnostics & Investment Targeting (p.3-4)

Grant GFDRR-LAC-2027-020 ($1.10M) ran CPRT tabletop stress-tests across Saint Lucia, Dominica, and Grenada, informing $145.0M in IDA regional preparedness financing.

## S3.0 M&E Emergency Readiness Gains (p.5-6)

Port-to-shelter relief dispatch time in joint CDEMA drills improved by 41%, covering 1,100,000 island residents (RF-2030-IND-07).

## S4.0 Disability Registry & Assistive Power Backup (p.7-8)

Municipal emergency registries added voluntary assistive-device power flags so oxygen-dependent and mobility-impaired residents receive priority pre-storm transport to solar shelters.
