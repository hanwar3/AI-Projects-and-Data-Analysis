## Executive Summary

Strictly Confidential FCV implementation and third-party monitoring assessment of wadi flash-flood storm channels, cholera-preventive drainage, and UNOPS delivery in Yemen.

## S1.0 Wadi Flash Floods & Waterborne Disease Compounding (p.1-2)

Extreme convective cloudbursts over Sailah wadi channels in Sana'a and Crater/Al-Mualla in Aden overwhelm war-damaged storm culverts, triggering acute cholera spikes in IDP neighbourhoods.

## S2.0 UN-Partnered Resilient Drainage & Preparedness Delivery (p.3-4)

Grant GFDRR-YEM-2027-022 ($2.05M) provided satellite damage verification and hydraulic rehabilitation designs informing $175.0M in IDA FCV Integrated Urban Services grants.

## S3.0 M&E Flood-Cholera Risk Reduction Metrics (p.5-6)

Rehabilitated stone-masonry flood channels and solar pumping stations protect 2,900,000 urban residents (RF-2030-IND-07) and cut post-storm standing-sewage duration by 67%.

## S4.0 Strictly Confidential UXO Clearance & Monitor Protocol (p.7-8)

STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL — AMS 6.21A Security & INT Safeguard: Unexploded ordnance (UXO) clearance certificates and encrypted satellite geotag verification hashes are required prior to contractor payment release across contested district boundaries.
