# DOC-GFDRR-010: Türkiye GRADE Rapid Seismic Loss Analytics & Public Building Retrofit Evaluation

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-TR-2026-010`
- **Country / Region:** Türkiye (`ECA`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 1: Strengthening Risk-Informed Decision Making
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Disaster Risk Analytics
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.90M / $650.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 5,600,000

## Executive Summary

Evaluation of GFDRR's Global Rapid post-disaster Damage Estimation (GRADE) workflow and combined seismic-plus-energy-efficiency retrofitting of municipal public buildings in Türkiye.

## S1.0 Rapid Remote Loss Estimation Within Two Weeks of Rupture (p.1-3)

Deploying the GRADE methodology combining ShakeMap ground-motion footprints, building census fragility curves, and high-resolution satellite damage proxies delivered sectoral direct economic loss estimates within 12 business days of seismic rupture—within 7.4% of subsequent ground-verified recovery audits.

## S2.0 Seismic & Energy Co-Benefit Public Building Pipeline (p.4-6)

Grant GFDRR-TR-2026-010 ($1.90M) ranked 2,400 hospitals, schools, and administrative centers using probabilistic life-safety and thermal-envelope criteria, informing $650.0M in IBRD Resilient Public Buildings financing.

## S3.0 M&E Structural Safety & Decarbonization Outcomes (p.7-8)

Retrofitted public facilities meet Immediate Occupancy life-safety standards under 475-year PGA design spectra while cutting annual heating/cooling energy demand by 34%, protecting 5,600,000 service users (RF-2030-IND-04).

## S4.0 Universal Accessibility & Childcare Continuity (p.9-10)

All retrofitted municipal service hubs integrate seismic non-structural bracing in daycare wings and accessible emergency egress signage.
