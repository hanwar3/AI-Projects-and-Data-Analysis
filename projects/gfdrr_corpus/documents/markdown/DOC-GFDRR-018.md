# DOC-GFDRR-018: Sahel Regional Digital Earth & impACT360 Cross-Border Flood-Conflict Analytics

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-AFR-2027-018`
- **Country / Region:** Sahel Regional (`AFR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 1: Strengthening Risk-Informed Decision Making
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Digital Earth
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** FCV
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.65M / $310.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 6,100,000

## Executive Summary

Operational Digital Earth and impACT360 remote-sensing workflow monitoring flood-induced cropland loss and transhumance corridor stress across inaccessible Sahel borderlands.

## S1.0 Remote Sensing Imperative in Inaccessible FCV Basins (p.1-2)

Field enumerator access is restricted across 64% of the Liptako-Gourma and Lake Chad tributary floodplains, creating blind spots for post-flood recovery allocation.

## S2.0 Sentinel-1 SAR + impACT360 Compound Risk Pipeline (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-AFR-2027-018 ($1.65M) fused all-weather Sentinel-1 SAR inundation footprints with GFDRR's impACT360 socio-economic vulnerability layers, informing $310.0M in regional IDA FCV resilience allocations.

## S3.0 M&E Turnaround Speed & Targeting Accuracy (p.6-7)

District flood-damage bulletins now reach World Bank task teams within 96 hours of peak inundation, guiding adaptive recovery for 6,100,000 cross-border residents (RF-2030-IND-01).

## S4.0 Do-No-Harm Aggregation Safeguards (p.8-9)

Publicly released rasters are spatially coarsened to 5 km hex cells in active conflict zones so pastoralist camp locations cannot be exploited by armed actors.
