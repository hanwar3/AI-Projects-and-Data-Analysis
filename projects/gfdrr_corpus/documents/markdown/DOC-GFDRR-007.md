## Executive Summary

Strictly Confidential operational security and preparedness assessment of rehabilitated Departmental Emergency Operations Centers (COUN/COUD) and humanitarian evacuation corridors in Haiti.

## S1.0 Multi-Hazard & Urban Fragility Compounding Risk (p.1-2)

Concurrent seismic hazard along the Enriquillo-Plantain Garden fault, tropical hurricane tracks, and acute urban gang-controlled transport bottlenecks severely constrain civil protection pre-positioning across Ouest, Sud, and Grand'Anse departments.

## S2.0 EOC Redundant Communications & Financing Informed (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-HT-2026-007 ($2.30M) financed satellite-mesh VHF backup links, seismic isolation of 6 Departmental EOCs, and Crisis Preparedness and Response Toolkit (CPRT) simulations, informing $195.0M in IDA emergency preparedness grants.

## S3.0 M&E Readiness & GBV Shelter Safeguard Metrics (p.6-7)

Emergency activation readiness scores rose from 41/100 to 79/100 across 6 departments, covering 2,700,000 exposed residents (RF-2030-IND-07) with mandatory GBV referral desks embedded in 100% of municipal shelter activation SOPs.
