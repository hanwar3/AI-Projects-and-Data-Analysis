# DOC-GFDRR-008: Tajikistan Glacial Mudflow & Flood-Resilient Bridge Reconstruction Analytics

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-TJ-2026-008`
- **Country / Region:** Tajikistan (`ECA`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Resilient Infrastructure
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** None
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.25M / $185.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 1,700,000

## Executive Summary

Debris-flow hydraulic scour analytics and resilient bridge pier redesign across Khatlon and Gorno-Badakhshan mountain transport corridors in Tajikistan.

## S1.0 Glacial Lake Outburst & Mudflow Corridor Exposure (p.1-2)

Accelerated Tien Shan and Pamir cryosphere melt triggered destructive spring mudflows that severed 24 regional highway bridges, isolating mountain districts from Dushanbe food and medical supply chains for up to 19 days.

## S2.0 Resilient Span Standards & World Bank Corridor Financing (p.3-4)

Grant GFDRR-TJ-2026-008 ($1.25M) introduced single-span clear-channel bridge designs, check-dam debris traps, and vegetated slope bio-engineering, informing $185.0M in IDA Resilient Transport Connectivity investments.

## S3.0 M&E Network Redundancy & Beneficiary Outcomes (p.5-6)

Expected annual economic disruption losses along the Vakhsh-Panj corridor declined by 61%, safeguarding lifelines for 1,700,000 users (RF-2030-IND-04).

## S4.0 Asset Management Sustainability & Road Agency Uptake (p.7-8)

Ministry of Transport integrated geohazard criticality scoring into its RAMS road asset budgeting database,ring-fencing 14% of annual maintenance funds for slope drainage.
