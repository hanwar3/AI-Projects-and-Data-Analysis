# DOC-GFDRR-021: Kenya Nairobi River Green Drainage, Slope Bio-Engineering & Resettlement Audit

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-KEN-2027-021`
- **Country / Region:** Kenya (`AFR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Nature-based Solutions for Climate Resilience
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Confidential`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.55M / $260.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 3,400,000

## Executive Summary

Confidential environmental-social and M&E review of green urban drainage swales, riparian park detention basins, and road-slope bio-engineering in Nairobi and Murang'a.

## S1.0 Urban Flash Runoff & Peri-Urban Landslide Disruption (p.1-2)

Impervious surface expansion in the Nairobi River basin increased 10-year peak urban runoff by 38%, inundating informal riparian wards and undermining commuter rail embankments.

## S2.0 Green Drainage & Transport Corridor Bio-Engineering (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-KEN-2027-021 ($1.55M) designed vegetated bioswales, constructed riparian wetlands, and vetiver road-cut stabilization, informing $260.0M in World Bank Urban Mobility & Flood Resilience financing.

## S3.0 M&E Peak Attenuation & Beneficiary Reach (p.6-7)

Sub-catchment peak discharge fell by 27% in instrumented pilot reaches, protecting 3,400,000 urban commuters and riparian residents (RF-2030-IND-02).

## S4.0 Confidential Riparian Livelihood Restoration Audit (p.8-9)

Classified Confidential pending Environmental and Social Framework (ESF) grievance settlement: 100% of 1,140 informal women micro-traders within the 30-meter riparian buffer must receive verified stall relocation compensation prior to civil works notice-to-proceed.
