# DOC-GFDRR-016: Philippines Subnational Typhoon Parametric Risk Pool & Adaptive Cash Linkage

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-PH-2027-016`
- **Country / Region:** Philippines (`EAP`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 3: Enhancing Financial Preparedness and Disaster Risk Finance
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Disaster Risk Finance
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.50M / $430.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 7,500,000

## Executive Summary

Evaluation of provincial catastrophe risk pooling and anticipatory social protection triggers for Super Typhoon wind and storm-surge corridors in eastern Visayas and Bicol.

## S1.0 Subnational Fiscal Exhaustion Under Sequential Typhoons (p.1-2)

Local Government Units (LGUs) in Bicol and Eastern Visayas routinely exhaust their 5% Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Funds by August, delaying emergency relief.

## S2.0 Parametric Provincial Pool & DPL Financing Informed (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-PH-2027-016 ($1.50M) structured multi-province parametric typhoon wind-speed payout rules and anticipatory cash protocols, informing $430.0M under the Philippines Disaster Risk Management & Climate DPL series.

## S3.0 M&E Anticipatory Payout Speed & Coverage (p.6-7)

Parametric LGU payouts settled in 8 days on average during FY2027 typhoon triggers, supporting 7,500,000 beneficiaries (RF-2030-IND-05) via Pantawid digital wallets.

## S4.0 Gender-Tagged Local DRRM Budget Execution (p.8-9)

88% of participating provinces adopted gender-tagged emergency procurement templates ensuring maternal health and safe-space supplies in pre-landfall payouts.
