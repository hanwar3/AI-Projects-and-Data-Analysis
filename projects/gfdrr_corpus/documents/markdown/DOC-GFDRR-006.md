# DOC-GFDRR-006: Mali Agro-Hydromet Last-Mile Early Warning & FCV Community Dissemination Review

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-ML-2026-006`
- **Country / Region:** Mali (`AFR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 4: Improving Disaster Preparedness and Resilient Recovery
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Hydromet Services and Early Warning Systems
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** FCV, Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.75M / $160.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 3,100,000

## Executive Summary

Operational evaluation of Mali-Meteo automated river/rain gauges, voice-SMS agro-climatic advisories, and women-led flood warning relays in FCV-affected Mopti and Segou regions.

## S1.0 Observation Network Gaps in Fragile Basins (p.1-2)

Armed insecurity across central Mali forced manual hydrological station closures along the Bani and Inner Niger Delta reaches, degrading 5-day flood lead-time skill below 48%.

## S2.0 Satellite-Blended Hydromet Modernization (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-ML-2026-006 ($1.75M, co-financed under CREWS) integrated tamper-resistant solar telemetry gauges with satellite rainfall-runoff ensembles, informing $160.0M in World Bank Hydromet & Food System Resilience financing.

## S3.0 M&E Early Warning Reach & Lead-Time Gains (p.6-8)

Tracked under Priority 4 indicator RF-2030-IND-06 (people covered by actionable multi-hazard early warning), 3,100,000 agropastoralists receive Bambara, Fulfulde, and Songhai rural radio and interactive voice response (IVR) alerts, extending flood lead time to 96 hours and reducing millet replanting losses by 29%.

## S4.0 FCV Field Focal Roster & Women's Relay Verification (p.9-10)

Women listeners' clubs constitute 52% of certified commune warning relays.
