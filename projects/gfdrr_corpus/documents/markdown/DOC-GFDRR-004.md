# DOC-GFDRR-004: Viet Nam Mekong Delta Mangrove Bioshield & Coastal Erosion NbS Evaluation

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-VN-2026-003`
- **Country / Region:** Viet Nam (`EAP`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Nature-based Solutions for Climate Resilience
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.40M / $315.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 4,900,000

## Executive Summary

Assessment of hybrid mangrove coastal bioshields and permeable bamboo T-fences attenuating typhoon wave energy along Ca Mau and Soc Trang coastlines in the Mekong Delta.

## S1.0 Coastal Subsidence & Mangrove Squeeze Diagnostics (p.1-3)

Upstream sediment trapping and groundwater extraction drive shoreline retreat exceeding 18 meters per year along unprotected segments of Ca Mau and Soc Trang, threatening sea dykes protecting intensive shrimp-mangrove livelihoods.

## S2.0 Hybrid Green-Grey Design & Investment Pipeline (p.4-6)

Grant GFDRR-VN-2026-003 ($1.40M) quantified wave-height attenuation of 55% to 72% across 250-meter Avicennia-Rhizophora mangrove belts paired with porous sediment-trapping fences, reducing concrete revetment toe-scour maintenance costs by 39% across a $315.0M World Bank coastal resilience lending operation.

## S3.0 M&E Results & Co-Benefit Valuation (p.7-8)

The operation safeguards 4,900,000 delta inhabitants under RF-2030-IND-02 and restores 9,800 hectares of estuarine mangrove habitat while increasing sustainable mud-crab and polyculture aquaculture yields by 24% for participating coastal cooperatives.

## S4.0 Inclusive Livelihoods & Ethnic Minority Participation (p.9-10)

Khmer ethnic minority and women-headed aquaculture households received 48% of mangrove co-management contracts, backed by SADDD livelihood baseline tracking.
