# DOC-GFDRR-013: Mozambique Safer Schools Cyclone Retrofit & Post-Storm Attendance M&E Review

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-MZ-2027-013`
- **Country / Region:** Mozambique (`AFR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Resilient Human Development Infra - Safer Schools & Health Systems
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** FCV, Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.70M / $280.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 4,200,000

## Executive Summary

Results evaluation of wind-resistant modular classroom standards and FCV displacement-sensitive school recovery across Sofala, Zambezia, and Cabo Delgado in Mozambique.

## S1.0 Recurrent Cyclone Classroom Loss & Dropout Risk (p.1-2)

Sequential Indian Ocean cyclones stripped roofs from over 4,100 classrooms in central Mozambique, while northern insurgency displacement doubled pupil-classroom ratios in host-district schools across Cabo Delgado.

## S2.0 Safer Schools Structural Package & IDA Leverage (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-MZ-2027-013 ($1.70M) calibrated 210 km/h cyclone roof-truss tie-down codes and rapid damage inspection apps, informing $280.0M in World Bank Safer Schools & Learning Recovery investments across 1,850 school complexes.

## S3.0 M&E Instructional Continuity & Beneficiary Count (p.6-7)

During the 2027 tropical storm season, retrofitted classrooms experienced <1.5% roof damage versus 34% in non-retrofitted control schools, cutting post-storm school closure days from 38 to 4 days and benefiting 4,200,000 students and surrounding community members.

## S4.0 Adolescent Girls' Retention & IDP Inclusion (p.8-9)

SADDD attendance tracking confirmed adolescent girls' post-cyclone re-enrollment reached 94.6% where menstrual hygiene WASH blocks and IDP catch-up tutoring were bundled.
