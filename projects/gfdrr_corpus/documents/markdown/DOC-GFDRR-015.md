# DOC-GFDRR-015: Nepal Municipal Seismic Building Code E-Permitting & Mason Compliance Audit

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-NP-2027-015`
- **Country / Region:** Nepal (`SAR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Building Regulation for Resilience - Resilient Housing
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Official Use Only`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2027
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.30M / $190.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 2,600,000

## Executive Summary

Internal operational evaluation of digital seismic building permit workflows and female mason certification across 42 municipalities in western Nepal's seismic gap.

## S1.0 Western Nepal Seismic Gap & Rapid Peri-Urban Growth (p.1-2)

Karnali and Sudurpashchim provinces sit astride a locked Himalayan megathrust segment unruptured since 1505, where 68% of new reinforced-concrete frame dwellings lacked ductile column-beam joint detailing.

## S2.0 Electronic Building Permit System & Grant Leverage (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-NP-2027-015 ($1.30M) deployed open-source municipal e-BPS compliance checks and certified 3,800 masons, informing $190.0M in World Bank Urban Governance & Resilience financing.

## S3.0 M&E Code Compliance & Beneficiary Metrics (p.6-7)

Field rebar inspection compliance rose from 39% to 84% across participating municipalities, covering 2,600,000 residents under RF-2030-IND-03.

## S4.0 Women Masons & Dalit Household Technical Assistance (p.8-9)

Women comprised 31% of newly licensed seismic masons, and free mobile structural clinics assisted 14,200 low-income and Dalit owner-builders.
