# DOC-GFDRR-001: Lower Indus Floodplain Nature-based Retention & Embankment Setback Assessment

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-PK-2026-001`
- **Country / Region:** Pakistan (`SAR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 2: Reducing Risk and Mainstreaming DRM
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Nature-based Solutions for Climate Resilience
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.85M / $410.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 6,400,000

## Executive Summary

Technical evaluation of floodplain wetland reconnection, Tori-Manchar retention basins, and vegetated embankment setbacks along the lower Indus plain in Sindh, informing $410M in World Bank flood resilience investment targeting 6.4 million residents.

## S1.0 Hydro-Geomorphic Baseline & Flood Pooling Dynamics (p.1-3)

Repeat inundation mapping from the 2010 riverine flood wave and the 2022 direct monsoon deluge demonstrates prolonged multi-month ponding trapped between the Kirthar piedmont and right-bank Indus embankments across Dadu, Qambar Shahdadkot, and Jamshoro districts. Conventional raised earth bunds without off-channel storage amplified peak water surface elevations by 0.42 to 0.68 meters during 50-year return period discharges.

## S2.0 Grant Intervention & World Bank Financing Informed (p.4-6)

Grant GFDRR-PK-2026-001 ($1.85M) deployed the GFDRR Nature-Based Solutions Opportunity Scan coupled with 2D hydrodynamic modelling over 30-meter terrain grids. The analytics directly informed $410.0M in IBRD/IDA financing under the Resilient Sindh Integrated Flood Management Project, reallocating 28% of grey canal-lining capital toward 14,500 hectares of seasonal floodplain retention wetlands and native tamarix riparian buffers.

## S3.0 M&E Results Framework & 2026-2030 Indicators (p.7-9)

Under GFDRR Results Framework indicator RF-2030-IND-02 (people with reduced flood risk from risk-informed investments), the combined grey-green embankment setback package targets 6,400,000 beneficiaries by FY2029, reducing modelled 25-year agricultural inundation duration from 49 days to 18 days. Benefit-cost ratio (BCR) across three wetland corridors was estimated at 3.4x under RCP4.5 monsoon intensification.

## S4.0 Inclusive DRM & Gender Equality Mainstreaming (p.10-11)

Community consultations recorded sex, age, and disability-disaggregated data (SADDD) across 42 riparian villages. Women-led wetland stewardship committees hold 45% voting seats in district water user associations and manage seasonal recession-fishery user permits, addressing historic exclusion of landless women sharecroppers during recovery.
