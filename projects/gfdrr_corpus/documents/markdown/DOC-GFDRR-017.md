- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Disaster Risk Analytics
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** FCV, Gender/Inclusion
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $1.20M / $1250.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 18,500,000

## Executive Summary

Corporate M&E methodology note defining GFDRR's 2026-2030 Results Framework indicators (RF-2030-IND-01 to IND-08), double-counting deduplication rules, and the 100 million climate-and-disaster-resilient beneficiaries target by 2030.

## S1.0 From 2021-2025 Umbrella Delivery to the 2026-2030 100M Target (p.1-3)

Building on the FY2022-FY2025 Umbrella Trust Fund baseline—where active grants grew from 134 to 328 and informed $22.19B in World Bank financing ($253 informed per $1 disbursed in FY2025; 76% gender-informed; 47% in FCV settings)—GFDRR's 2026-2030 Strategy commits to measurably improving climate and disaster resilience for 100 million people by 2030 across 4 Priority Areas, 10 Thematic Areas, and 2 Crosscutting Pillars.

## S2.0 Standardized Outcome Indicators RF-2030-IND-01 to IND-08 (p.4-6)

Core corporate indicators comprise: RF-2030-IND-01 (population covered by open risk analytics and Digital Earth screening), RF-2030-IND-02 (beneficiaries protected by NbS and urban heat interventions), RF-2030-IND-03 (people in code-compliant resilient housing), RF-2030-IND-04 (users of resilient lifeline transport, schools, and health systems), RF-2030-IND-05 (population covered by sovereign/subnational DRF instruments), RF-2030-IND-06 (people receiving last-mile Hydromet/EWS alerts), RF-2030-IND-07 (EOC preparedness coverage), and RF-2030-IND-08 (share of closed grants meeting dual FCV + SADDD Gender/Inclusion benchmarks).

## S3.0 Spatial Deduplication & Attribution Rules (p.7-9)

To prevent portfolio inflation toward the 100M target, Admin-2 spatial intersection rules cap overlapping grant counts at the maximum single-hazard exposed population per district unless independent structural plus financial layers are empirically verified. Only World Bank operations reaching Board approval with explicit PAD citation to GFDRR analytics qualify as 'Financing Informed'.

## S4.0 Crosscutting FCV & Gender/Inclusion Scoring Rubric (p.10-12)

A grant qualifies as Gender-Substantive under RF-2030-IND-08 only when it includes (i) gap analysis using SADDD, (ii) targeted design actions (such as women-led EWS committees or joint housing title), and (iii) a tracked M&E gender outcome indicator. FCV-tagged grants must additionally document conflict-sensitivity and third-party monitoring safeguards.
