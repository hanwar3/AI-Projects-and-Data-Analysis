# DOC-GFDRR-011: Bhutan Digital Earth Geohazard Platform & Municipal Risk Screening Review

- **Grant ID:** `GFDRR-BT-2026-011`
- **Country / Region:** Bhutan (`SAR`)
- **GFDRR Priority Area:** Priority 1: Strengthening Risk-Informed Decision Making
- **GFDRR Thematic Area:** Digital Earth
- **Crosscutting Pillars:** None
- **WB Classification:** `Public`
- **Fiscal Year:** FY2026
- **Grant Disbursed / WB Financing Informed:** $0.90M / $95.0M
- **Targeted Resilience Beneficiaries:** 550,000

## Executive Summary

Open geospatial data cube integrating LiDAR slope stability, Sentinel-1 InSAR deformation, and GLOF inundation layers for risk-informed municipal capital planning in Bhutan.

## S1.0 Fragmented Hazard Basemaps in Steep Himalayan Dzongkhags (p.1-2)

Prior to FY2026, landslide susceptibility, riverine flood return periods, and seismic microzonation layers resided in incompatible desktop GIS silos across four ministries.

## S2.0 Digital Earth Stack & Open GeoNode Architecture (p.3-5)

Grant GFDRR-BT-2026-011 ($0.90M) operationalized an open OGC-compliant Digital Earth risk platform ingesting Sentinel-1 InSAR slope-creep time series and 5-meter DEMs, informing $95.0M in World Bank urban and hydropower corridor investments.

## S3.0 M&E Institutional Adoption & Screening Uptake (p.6-7)

All 20 dzongkhag engineering cells now run automated multi-hazard site screening prior to public procurement approval, covering 550,000 citizens (RF-2030-IND-01).

## S4.0 Civil Servant Geospatial Capacity Transfer (p.8-9)

112 Bhutanese engineers completed hands-on Python raster and GeoNode administration certification, eliminating reliance on external vendor licenses.
