01

Who gets federal hazard mitigation money, and for what?

Do FEMA's three mitigation grant streams fund different projects, and what drives the size of an award?

Data
OpenFEMA Hazard Mitigation Assistance Projects v4 (56,410 records, pulled 3 Oct 2026); sample of 7,781 approved physical projects, FY2015–2024
Programs
HMGP (post-disaster), BRIC/PDM (pre-disaster competitive), FMA (flood insurance)
Methods
χ² test with Cramér's V and adjusted residuals; Levene, Welch ANOVA, Kruskal–Wallis, Tukey HSD; OLS on log award with region and year fixed effects, VIF, Breusch–Pagan, Jarque–Bera, HC3 and state-clustered errors
Tools
Python, pandas, SciPy, statsmodels, Matplotlib, Jupyter
Notebook with code Result tables (CSV)
  • $7.83B

    federal share obligated across 7,781 projects. Florida, Texas, Louisiana, California and Puerto Rico together received 53% of it.

  • V = 0.36

    The project mix depends strongly on the program (χ²(10) = 1,970, p < 0.001). FMA is dominated by home elevations and buyouts, HMGP carries most generator and warning projects, and BRIC/PDM leans to safe rooms and other structural work.

  • +96%

    A BRIC/PDM award is about twice the size of an HMGP award for the same project type, region and year (95% CI +67% to +130%; holds with state-clustered errors, p = 0.001).

Pakistan earthquakes: where they start vs. where they are felt

Does an epicentre map show where people are exposed to shaking?

Data
USGS ComCat (12,715 earthquakes, M ≥ 2.5, 1902–2025); USGS ShakeMap grids for 309 events; AWS Terrain Tiles; UN OCHA boundaries
Methods
Kernel density, cell-wise maximum MMI composite, Gutenberg–Richter b-value, completeness check
Tools
Python, NumPy, SciPy, DuckDB, Matplotlib, custom oblique renderer
Report (PDF, 11 pp.) Code
  • 61 vs 840

    epicentres in the Punjab plain vs. the Sulaiman–Kirthar belt at the same latitudes.

  • 98.4%

    of Pakistan has still felt at least MMI IV since 2000, and 28.9% has felt MMI VI or more. Lahore's strongest shaking came from a rupture 340 km away.

03

Texas: flood damage paid, climate, earthquakes

Where has flood damage actually been paid out, and how does that sit against climate and seismicity?

Data
OpenFEMA NFIP redacted claims 2000–2025 (272,924 claims, $16.0B paid); USGS ComCat; TerraClimate 2000–2021; Census TIGER/Line
Methods
Paged API pull, payout-weighted density with log stretch, bivariate colour classes
Tools
Python, requests, NumPy, SciPy, xarray, oblique-relief-maps skill
Code

The flood map shows realised, insured damage rather than modelled hazard, so uninsured rural floodplains can appear blank and still flood. Harris County dwarfs every other cluster, so density is log-stretched to keep Beaumont, the Rio Grande Valley, Central Texas and Dallas–Fort Worth visible.

04

Pakistan floods, 2010 and 2022

How do the two largest floods on record sit on the Indus terrain?

Data
UN OCHA 2010 inundation; UNOSAT / VIIRS 2022 extent (375 m); Copernicus GLO-30 DEM; Natural Earth rivers; HDX boundaries
Methods
Vector rasterisation onto the DEM grid, elevation-weighted inundation layer, ocean masking
Tools
Python, rasterio, GeoPandas, SciPy, Pillow, QGIS
Code

The 2010 flood travelled as a wave down the Indus from Khyber Pakhtunkhwa to Sindh. In 2022 rain fell directly on Sindh and eastern Balochistan and pooled for months between the Kirthar range and the river embankments. Both footprints concentrate on the flat lower Indus plain, the area I worked on for nature-based flood solutions at WWF-Pakistan.

05

Pakistan temperature × rainfall

Where is it hot, wet, or both?

Data
TerraClimate monthly grids, ~4 km, 2000–2021
Methods
Climate normals, regridding to terrain, bivariate classes, log-scaled rainfall
Tools
Python, xarray, adlfs, NumPy, oblique-relief-maps skill
Code

Four named corners: cool and dry (Karakoram, Chagai), hot and dry (Thar, lower Indus, Balochistan), cool and wet (northern mountain fringe), hot and wet (the Punjab and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa monsoon belt).

oblique-relief-maps: a Claude skill

SKILL.md, references (cartography, data sources, pitfalls), scripts (terrain, shading, renderer)
Tools

I wrote it after the Pakistan earthquake maps. With it installed, the Pakistan climate map and the Texas series (sheets 03 and 05) were each produced from a short request in the same style. It starts by asking whether a map should show where events originate or where they are felt, the check behind sheet 02.
